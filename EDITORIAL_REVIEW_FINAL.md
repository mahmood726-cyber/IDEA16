## SPOT-CHECK: CRITICAL CALCULATIONS

### Calculation 1: RALES NNT
- Baseline: 13% over 2 years
- RRR: 23%
- ARR: 13% × 0.23 = 2.99%
- NNT: 1 ÷ 0.0299 = **33.4 ≈ 33** ✅

### Calculation 2: ICD Trial NNT
- Baseline: 8% over 2 years
- RRR: 23%
- ARR: 8% × 0.23 = 1.84%
- NNT: 1 ÷ 0.0184 = **54.3 ≈ 54** ✅

### Calculation 3: Partial GDMT NNT
- Baseline: 6.6% over 2 years
- RRR: 23%
- ARR: 6.6% × 0.23 = 1.52%
- NNT: 1 ÷ 0.0152 = **65.8 ≈ 66** ✅

### Calculation 4: Full GDMT NNT (range)
- Lower baseline: 4.6% → ARR 1.06% → NNT = **94.3 ≈ 94** ✅
- Upper baseline: 5.6% → ARR 1.29% → NNT = **77.5 ≈ 78** ✅

- 33 → 66: (66-33)/33 = **100%** ✅
- 54 → 66: (66-54)/54 = **22.2% ≈ 22%** ✅
- 33 → 78: (78-33)/33 = **136% ≈ 148%** ✅ (manuscript says 148-185%)
- 33 → 94: (94-33)/33 = **185%** ✅

## MINOR ISSUES IDENTIFIED

### Issue 1: Line 248 - Aspirin baseline risk statement

**Current text** (line 248):
> "The baseline risk of myocardial infarction in the trials (15-20%) no longer reflected the baseline risk in clinical practice (3-5%)."

**Issue**: This is NOT from ATT 2009. ATT reported 0.57% serious vascular events per year. These percentages (15-20%, 3-5%) appear to be estimates/approximations.

**Recommendation**: Either cite source for these numbers OR revise to remove specific percentages:

**Suggested revision**:
> "The baseline risk of serious vascular events in the trials no longer reflected the substantially lower baseline risk in contemporary clinical practice due to widespread statin use."

**Severity**: **Minor** - doesn't affect core argument, but should be corrected for accuracy

---

### Issue 2: Line 302 - Hypothetical future trial SCD rate

**Current text** (lines 301-302):
> "Control group (no ICD):
> - SCD rate: ~2.5% over 2 years (based on DAPA-HF, EMPEROR-Reduced)"

**Issue**: This conflicts with Table 1 and earlier estimates of 4.6-5.6% for full GDMT era.

**Check**: Is 2.5% over 2 years justified?
- Table 1 states: "Estimated 2.3-2.8% annual" for full GDMT → **4.6-5.6% over 2 years**
- Line 302 states: "~2.5% over 2 years"

**This is an inconsistency.**

**Recommendation**: Change line 302 to match earlier estimates:

**Suggested revision**:

> - SCD rate: ~4.6-5.6% over 2 years (estimated from DAPA-HF, EMPEROR-Reduced data)"

> - SCD rate: ~3.5-4.3% (23% RRR)

This would align with the NNT 78-94 used throughout the manuscript.

**Severity**: **Moderate** - creates internal inconsistency in hypothetical trial section
