### **Figure 4: DANISH Trial Competing Risks** 🔴 NEW
**Purpose**: Shows your key evidence (DANISH = aspirin moment)
**Message**: "ICD reduces SCD but provides NO survival benefit"

**Design**: Dual Kaplan-Meier curves
- **Panel A (SCD)**: Clear separation
  - Control: 8.2% SCD at 5 years
  - ICD: 4.3% SCD at 5 years
  - HR 0.50, P<0.01 ✓ Significant

- **Panel B (Mortality)**: Minimal separation
  - Control: 23.4% deaths at 5 years
  - ICD: 21.6% deaths at 5 years
  - HR 0.87, P=0.28 ✗ Not significant

**Key insight**:
- SCD reduced by 3.9% (8.2% → 4.3%)
- But non-SCD deaths increased by 2.1% (15.2% → 17.3%)
- Net effect: 1.8% mortality reduction (NOT significant)
- **Competing risks**: Patients saved from SCD died from other causes

**Data source**: DANISH trial (Køber NEJM 2016), N=1,116

**Why critical**:
1. DANISH is your smoking gun (like ARRIVE/ASCEND/ASPREE for aspirin)
2. Currently buried in text — figure makes it visually compelling
3. Competing risks is complex concept — visual makes it obvious
4. Anticipates reviewer questions ("What were actual DANISH results?")
5. Strengthens aspirin parallel (both show "no benefit in modern era")

**Status**: ✅ Complete specification in `figures/figure4_danish_competing_risks.md`
